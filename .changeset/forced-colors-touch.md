Windows contrast themes (forced colours): a pressed `Toggle`, the chosen `SegmentedControl` segment, `Radio`, `Checkbox` and `Switch` state, the active `Tabs` bar, `Progress`, `Slider` and `RangeSlider` fills, `Pagination`'s current page, `Calendar`'s selected day, `Tree`, `NavSidebar` and `Table` selections, a highlighted menu, `Combobox` or `CommandPalette` row and `Stepper`'s steps are painted in the user's `Highlight` colour instead of vanishing; `Tooltip`, `Toast` and `Drawer` keep an edge.

Touch: hover styles apply only where the device can hover, so a tapped `Button`, `Card`, `Link` or row no longer stays painted. `Dialog`, `Drawer` and the `Toast` region keep clear of the notch and the home indicator when the app uses `viewport-fit=cover`.
